Why ThirdEye Matters

In the critical moments of a criminal investigation, an accurate description of a suspect is paramount. Traditional sketch artistry is a rare skill and can be a bottleneck in the justice process. ThirdEye empowers law enforcement agencies by democratizing this capability.

Our platform provides a standardized, efficient, and highly effective tool to construct accurate facial composites based on witness descriptions. By leveraging artificial intelligence for recognition, we accelerate the identification process, increase accuracy, and bring perpetrators to justice faster.

Core Features

🕵

Interactive Sketch Constructor

Build detailed facial composites with an intuitive drag-and-drop interface. Choose from a vast library of facial features to create an accurate representation.

🧠

AI-Powered Recognition

Upload a constructed sketch or a hand-drawn image to scan a secure database. Our AI model provides potential matches with similarity scores in seconds.

🖼

Photo-to-Sketch Converter

Automatically convert any photograph into a high-quality line sketch, perfect for testing the system or for use in the recognition module.

🔐

Secure & Role-Based

With distinct roles for Admins (managing data) and Users (forensic work), our system ensures data integrity and secure access control.

Our Process

1
Construct or Upload

Use the Sketch Constructor to build a composite from eyewitness details, or upload an existing hand-drawn sketch or converted photo.

2
Initiate AI Search

Submit the sketch to the Face Recognition module. Our deep learning model analyzes the key facial geometry and features.

3
Analyze Matches

Review a ranked list of potential matches from the database, complete with similarity scores and suspect details, to guide the investigation.
